Privacy

Almost nothing to tell.

Last updated September 7, 2026

Glyph is a note-taking and tutoring app for students, built by Vaibhav Gollapalli and Sudeep Senivarapu. This page covers the waitlist website at glyphedu.tech. It's short because we collect almost nothing.

What we collect

Your email address, if you choose to join the waitlist. That's it. We don't collect names, locations, or anything else, and the site works fine if you never type anything.

What we use it for

One confirmation email when you sign up, and updates about Glyph — like when the app opens up. Nothing else. We never sell or share your email with anyone, and we don't send marketing on anyone else's behalf.

Where it's stored

Waitlist emails are stored in a private Google Sheet and processed by Resend, the service that delivers our emails. Both act only on our instructions.

Cookies and tracking

None. This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and includes no trackers. Fonts and video are served from this site, not a third party. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log standard technical data (like IP addresses) to serve the page — see GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The waitlist isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you're a parent and believe your child under 13 signed up, email us and we'll delete it.

Removing your email

Reply to any email we send you, or write to hello@glyphedu.tech, and we'll delete your address. No questions, no delay.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll note it here with a new date. When the Glyph app launches, it will have its own privacy policy covering the app itself.

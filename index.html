How it works

Write. Circle. Learn.

  1. 01

    Write like you always have.

    Ruled, dotted, or blank. Your handwriting, your pace, an Apple Pencil canvas that stays out of the way. Glyph reads every line as you write it, on-device.

    Ink is read as ink, on-device

  2. 02

    Circle anything.

    Lasso a term, a step, a whole problem. A capsule rises under it — Explain, Hint, Check my work — and it knows what you circled because it read the page.

    Explain · Hint · Check my work

  3. 03

    It teaches. It never just tells.

    Steps written out like a tutor on paper. A worksheet aimed at the exact spot you slipped. A quiz later, from your own pages — because that's how things stick.

    Steps first · answer last

The stance

Built to make you learn, not skip.

Glyph never just hands you the answer. It walks the steps, shows the picture, and quizzes you later — because the research is clear: that's how things actually stick.

Rule 01Never just the answer.

Explain, hint, check — the answer is last, and it comes with the working.

Rule 02Retrieval, built in.

Flashcards and quizzes come from your own pages, on a schedule that remembers for you.

Rule 03Your notes stay yours.

Reading and tutoring run on-device. Taking notes is free, forever.
